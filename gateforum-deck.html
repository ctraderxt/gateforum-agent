THE RESEARCH FLOOR

How 12 Agents Decide

🔍 PHASE 1: Research (4 Analysts — Parallel)

📊 Market Analyst 📰 News Analyst 💬 Social Analyst 📈 Fundamentals Analyst → Fetch live data from Gate.com, CoinGecko, news APIs

⚔️ PHASE 2: Analysis (Bull vs Bear → Research Judge)

🐂 Bull Researcher ⟷ 🐻 Bear Researcher → ⚖️ Research Judge → Investment plan with direction

🛡️ PHASE 3: Risk Assessment (3 Risk Analysts)

🔥 Aggressive → ⚖️ Neutral → 🛡️ Conservative → Sizing + risk view

✅ PHASE 4: Final Verdict (Risk Judge gate → Trader)

🏛️ Risk Judge → BUY / SELL / HOLD + confidence + rationale + full transcript → 💼 Trader → Executes via Hummingbot
🧠 Self-Contained Engine (no stale fork) — 12 agents, zero GPU
⏱ ~3 min per full cycle (3 pairs in parallel)
🛡️ HOLD needs ≥2 of 3 risk analysts

Inspired by TauricResearch's multi-agent trading research — adapted for Condor and heavily customized.

STRATEGY STACK

Condor Agent · Routines · Hummingbot

🧠 Condor AI Agent — The Brain

LLM-powered decision engine. Runs every 15 min via TickEngine. AGENT.md + strategy.md define the complete playbook — a tick checklist that reads research verdicts, applies the confidence floor, opens positions on every actionable pair (max 3), and journals every decision with cooldowns.

⏱ Tick Loop (15 min) 📖 AGENT.md + strategy.md 🔍 Verdict Filtering 📊 Position Sizing 📝 Journaling + Cooldowns

🔧 Condor Routines — The Data Pipeline

Deterministic Python helpers. Auto-discovered by Condor. Three routines handle all data plumbing — the agent focuses purely on decisions.

gateforum_init.py → Launch research server gateforum_data.py → Fetch K-lines + CoinGecko gateforum_research.py → Run 12-agent research

🗳️ GateForum Research Server — The Council

Self-contained FastAPI + OpenAI-compatible engine. No LangGraph, no heavy framework, zero GPU. Renders the full bull/bear/risk-judge transcript for every research cycle.

localhost:8500 /research · /health 12-role research transcript out

⚡ Hummingbot + Gate.com — The Execution

Handles all trade execution via Gate.com Perpetuals. Isolated by controller ID. Platform-enforced stop loss, take profit, trailing stop and time limit — the agent can never open an unprotected position. The agent never touches order execution directly.

Position Executor → SL 2% / TP 4% / 1h limit → Trailing Stop 1→2% → Gate.com Perpetuals

Three routines + a research server handle all data plumbing and research orchestration. Agent focuses on decisions. Hummingbot handles execution.

RISK FRAMEWORK

Research-Validated · Platform-Enforced Defense

🛡️ Layer 1: Research Consensus

✓ Confidence ≥ 65% to enter

✓ 3 risk analysts assess every verdict

✓ Risk judge's HOLD needs ≥2 of 3 analysts to agree

✓ A lone veto is overruled — no single agent blocks a trade

📊 Layer 2: Position Sizing

✓ Max 3 positions concurrently

✓ $8–10 notional per position (test mode)

✓ 1× leverage (test) · 2× hard cap (race)

✓ 8% drawdown → scale down, keep trading (not a hard stop)

🎯 Layer 3: Exit Rules — every position protected

✓ 2% stop loss (platform-enforced)

✓ 4% take profit

✓ 1h max time limit — positions always cycle

✓ Trailing stop 1% → 2% locks in winners

✓ Every 30 min: close losers, 15-min cooldown, re-evaluate

🧠

Self-Healing

A 15-min watchdog monitors the whole stack — restarts services, resumes sessions, and even closes orphaned positions automatically. It runs for 48h with no human.

WHY THIS WINS

Edges Over Single-Agent Strategies

🏛️

Council, Not Single Mind

12 specialized AI agents research every trade. Bull vs Bear. Three risk perspectives. One model can hallucinate — a council catches errors.

🧠

Self-Contained & Lean

A self-contained research engine (OpenAI-compatible, zero GPU) replaces the stale LangGraph fork. No fragile dependencies — it just runs.

📜

Full Transparency

Every decision has a complete research transcript. Bull case, bear case, risk arguments — judges see the AI's reasoning in real time.

🔄

Adaptive Confidence

Conviction scores drive entries. Strong disagreement = lower confidence = stay out. No forced trades.

🔺

Risk Triangulation

Three perspectives assess before the risk judge rules. Aggressive, Neutral, Conservative — a committee, not a formula. A veto needs analyst consensus.

📊

Multi-Source Intelligence

Price + news + social + fundamentals. Sees what pure price models can't. ETF flows, exchange reserves, sentiment shifts, supply shocks.

🌍

Cross-Asset Diversification

BTC (crypto) + XAU (gold) + CL (oil). Three uncorrelated markets. When crypto chops, commodities still move.
